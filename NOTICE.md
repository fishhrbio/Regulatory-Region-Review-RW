This project presents a secondary analysis of published chromatin-state
annotations. The experimental measurements were generated by the ENCODE
Consortium, Broad Institute and Bradley E. Bernstein laboratory. The original
ChromHMM segmentation was produced by the Manolis Kellis computational biology
group and reported by Ernst et al. (2011), DOI 10.1038/nature09906.

UCSC distributes the included hg19 tracks, converted from the original hg18
annotations. The data retain their original reuse terms; see `data/SOURCES.md`.
Original project code and documentation are covered by `LICENSE`. Please retain
the applicable copyright notice and cite the original data investigators and
accessions when reusing the data or results.

The student affiliation identifies the project owner and does not imply
institutional endorsement.
